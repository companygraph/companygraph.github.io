A meta-model for operating a company — the structure its knowledge takes, so people and agents can both rely on it.

The vision

Written once, read by both.

We are working toward companies whose knowledge is written once, in a structure that the people who run them and the agents working beside them can both rely on — so that what a company knows can be read, checked and acted on where it is needed, rather than reassembled out of wikis, decks, tickets and chat threads every time somebody asks.

What we hold to

Three values, each with the thing we never do.

Adoption is not taxedWe never let the model cost more the more of a company, or the more of its agents, that reads it. Describe the pattern, never the companyWe never put a company's name, its tooling or its counts into the vocabulary. Run on what we publishWe never ship a type we have not already had to use on ourselves.
