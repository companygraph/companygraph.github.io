| 1a | The question is not about the model | It is answered with one sentence saying what the chat is for, and no tool is asked |

Where it departs

Where the pack does not do what its sources do, its README says so and says why.

rests onconcept Pack
  • must, must not, mayRFC 2119’s must, should and may: a requirement has a should, and a must not is a must written in the statement
  • One table of guarantees with a kind columntwo sections under Cockburn’s own headings, minimal and success
  • Black-box and white-box scopedropped: the steps show how much of the inside a scenario reaches
  • Goal levels as icons, five of themCockburn’s words, and the lowest left out, since his advice for a goal that low is not to write it
  • An underlined step calls a use casethe step’s actor resolves, and the use case it calls is drawn from includes and named in the action
  • Priority, satisfaction, conflicts, historynone: each moves or judges one requirement against another
  • spec-kit’s measurable success criteriona requirement, of kind quality where it measures a quality, its measure the fit criterion

How the pack joins core

A use case asks something of a feature and its actors are seats. A requirement applies to a feature, a product or a use case and follows a rule. Every edge runs one way and is optional.
