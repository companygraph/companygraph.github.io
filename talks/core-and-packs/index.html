Taking a pack

init --pack when you start, upgrade --pack later: vendored beside core, listed in the manifest, checked and moved with it.

rests onconcept Instanceconcept Releaseconcept Pin
$ companygraph upgrade --pack organization

packs: organization, vendored beside core
