## Writing rules
- The tagline says what someone can now do, in their words, and never names a screen, a service or a vendor.
- The H1 names the capability, not the vendor that supplies it.
- The page says nothing about a release, a ticket or a delivery date: those move, and a feature outlives all three.

Fixed and replaceable

What stays is what you wrote. What changes is what reads it, and each says whether it is replaceable today or by design.

Six practices

  1. Keep knowledge where no model owns it.
  2. Let code decide what code can decide.
  3. Ask typed questions before free text.
  4. A model proposes, a person decides.
  5. Pin every model, and measure before you move it.
  6. Ask before data leaves the machine.

What stays

The model you will change most often is the one you should depend on least.

Then the next model is a pin you move, not a project you start.
