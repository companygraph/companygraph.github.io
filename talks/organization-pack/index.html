Who directs an agent

Where it departs

Where the pack does not do what its sources do, its README says so, and why.

rests onconcept Pack
  • The two linesnamed by the right each carries, disciplinary and professional; “functional line” is used for neither
  • One reportsTotwo lines, as SAP, HR-XML and German practice keep them apart
  • A superior on the personnone: the lines run through the people a unit names and the units they sit in
  • Organizational unitgroup, because a team drawn across units is a group and not a unit
  • A position objecta row: of People while someone holds it, of Openings while it is open
  • The staff flag on each uniton the group's kind, and a staff person's Place is Staff
